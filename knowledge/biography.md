## Medium bio (~130 words, EN)

Chile, with a participation scholarship.

## Long bio (EN)

Manuel Josué Malla Campoverde is a technologist from Machala, Ecuador,
focused on applied machine learning and computer vision. He is completing a
B.Sc. in Information Technology at Universidad Técnica de Machala (2022–2027),
complemented by a summer program at FGV's Applied Mathematics School in Rio de
Janeiro (2024) — covering recommendation systems, optimization in Julia,
complex networks in epidemiology, and Bayesian regression — and the Data
Driven Decision Specialist program at ESPOL in Guayaquil (2025–2026), which he
attended on scholarship.

In industry, he works at LarvIA as a machine learning engineer, designing and
deploying ML pipelines for shrimp farming optimization in Ecuador and Latin
America: instance segmentation models for shrimp feed imagery, photogrammetry
with OpenCV for metric measurements from smartphone images, and computer
vision models running on edge devices. In research, he was a Machine Learning
& Data Science Research Intern at CNPEM (Brazilian Nanotechnology National
Laboratory) in Campinas, Brazil, analyzing high-density electrochemical sensor
data with unsupervised clustering and multi-label classification. Earlier, at
UTMACH's IT department, he designed SISCA, an intelligent access control and
security system for university classrooms with real-time facial
authentication.

His independent projects include MangroveNet (U²-Net multispectral
segmentation of mangrove ecosystems from Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery),
TreeSeg-ALS (deep-learning individual tree detection in airborne LiDAR,
reproducibility code for a manuscript submitted to *Forests*, MDPI), and a
comparative geoclustering study of homicides in Ecuador using KMeans, DBSCAN,
and HDBSCAN. He presented at the LatinX in CV undergraduate consortium at
CVPR 2024 (best presentation award, travel scholarship from the Computer
Vision Foundation) and spoke at FLISOL 2026 on small-object detection with
YOLO and SAHI. He is a Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador, a student
representative in UTMACH's Higher Education Assembly, and has taught Python
fundamentals to young students in Huaquillas. He speaks Spanish (native),
English (C1), and Portuguese (B1).
