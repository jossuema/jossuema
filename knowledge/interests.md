# Interests

What I gravitate toward — useful for statements of purpose and "research
interests" fields. Grounded in the work in
[`experience.md`](experience.md), [`projects/`](projects/) and
[`research/`](research/).

## Research interests

- **Computer vision for the physical world:** instance segmentation,
  small-object detection (YOLO + SAHI), photogrammetry and metric
  measurement from consumer cameras.
- **Remote sensing & environmental monitoring:** multispectral segmentation
  of mangrove ecosystems (MangroveNet), individual tree detection in
  airborne LiDAR (TreeSeg-ALS).
- **ML on the edge:** deploying and optimizing vision models on
  resource-constrained devices (LarvIA, SISCA's on-device face
  recognition).
- **ML for scientific instrumentation:** unsupervised structure discovery
  and multi-label classification on high-density electrochemical sensor
  data (CNPEM).
- **Spatial data science:** geoclustering and density-based methods
  (KMeans, DBSCAN, HDBSCAN) applied to public-policy problems such as
  homicide patterns in Ecuador.
