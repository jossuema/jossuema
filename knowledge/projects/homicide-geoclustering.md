# Geoclustering of homicides in Ecuador (2025)

A comparative analysis with KMeans, DBSCAN, and HDBSCAN.

- **Source:** [`../sources/cv-2026-08.md`](../sources/cv-2026-08.md)
- **Area:** spatial data science, unsupervised learning, public policy

## What it is

Research using government homicide data to identify location clusters where
homicides spread with a certain density and follow similar patterns,
comparing **KMeans, DBSCAN and HDBSCAN**.

## Reuse notes

- Strong example of data science applied to a pressing national problem —
  works well in scholarship essays about impact in Ecuador.
- Candidate for publication or a blog post if results are consolidated.
