# References & recommenders

People who can write recommendation letters, what they can credibly speak
to, and lead time they need. Most scholarships require 2–3 letters — keep
this current so no application stalls waiting to remember who to ask.

Template per recommender:

```markdown
## Name — Title, Institution
- **Contact:** email
- **Relationship:** supervisor/professor/mentor, during <dates>
- **Can speak to:** research ability / engineering / academics / leadership
- **Letters written:** <application, date> (keep copies in sources/)
- **Lead time:** e.g. 3 weeks
```
